gmail-backup – Privacy policy

gmail-backup accesses only the Gmail account of its owner, with the read-only scope gmail.readonly, solely to create a personal backup copy of that mailbox.

The downloaded messages are stored only on servers and devices controlled by the owner. No data is sold, shared with or transferred to any third party, and none is used for advertising, analytics or training of AI models.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
